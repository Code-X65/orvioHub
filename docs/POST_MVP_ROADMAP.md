# Orviohub Post-MVP Scope & Deferred Capabilities

This document provides explicit clarity regarding features and functional modules deferred beyond the initial MVP release.

---

## 1. Deferred Inventory Capabilities (Real Business Engine)
The MVP release provides an **Interactive Inventory Demo Dashboard** focusing on multi-branch switching, tenant isolation, and team management. The following core commerce capabilities are planned for subsequent phases:
- Real product catalog SKU variants and barcode scanning hardware integration.
- Real-time stock movement, physical inventory audits, and reorder point alerts.
- Live point-of-sale cash registers and physical receipt printer drivers.
- Supplier purchase orders, accounts payable, and customer receivables ledgers.

---

## 2. Deferred Multi-Application Modules
The following modular applications are registered as `coming_soon` and will be activated progressively:
- **`pos`**: Full Retail & Restaurant Point of Sale.
- **`booking`**: Appointments, calendar scheduling, and staff rostering.
- **`gym`**: Membership passes, recurring access cards, and turnstile integrations.
- **`taskmanagement`**: Internal team task assignments and kanban workflows.

---

## 3. Enterprise & Advanced Infrastructure
- Single Sign-On (SAML / SCIM / Okta enterprise federation).
- Dedicated read-replica data warehousing and offline sync engines.
