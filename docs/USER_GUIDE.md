# Orviohub User Guide

Welcome to **Orviohub**, the unified operational platform for modern businesses.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating an Account
1. Visit the signup page and register with your work email, name, and Nigerian phone number (`+234...`).
2. Verify your email address via the one-time verification link.
3. Complete your personal profile onboarding.

### 1.2 Creating Your First Organization
1. From the organization launcher, click **Create Organization**.
2. Enter your Organization Name (e.g. "Lagos Ventures") and select your primary operating state.
3. Your new organization will automatically start on a **30-Day Free Trial** with a pre-configured **Primary Demo Branch** under the **Inventory Management** app.
4. *Note*: Each user is entitled to **one Free Trial organization** and can own a maximum of **3 organizations total**.

---

## 2. Navigating the Inventory Demo Dashboard

> [!NOTE]
> In this MVP release, the Inventory application is in **Interactive Demo Mode**. You can explore branch switching, team assignments, and dashboard layouts. Real stock valuation and live sales transactions will be introduced in post-MVP releases.

### 2.1 Branch Management
- **Switching Branches**: Use the branch dropdown in the top navigation bar to switch between locations.
- **Adding a Branch**:
  - Click **Settings** > **Branches** > **Add Branch**.
  - Provide a Branch Name, Code, and Location.
  - *Branch Quotas*:
    - **Free Trial**: 1 Branch
    - **Standard Plan**: 3 Branches
    - **Premium Plan**: 10 Branches
- **Designating a Primary Branch**: Click the action menu next to any active branch and select **Set as Primary**.

### 2.2 Inviting Team Members
1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Team Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member**, enter their email address, and choose an Organization Role:
   - **Owner**: Full administrative and billing authority.
   - **Admin**: Day-to-day management of branches and apps.
   - **Member**: Standard operational access.
   - **Viewer**: Read-only reporting access.
3. Once joined, you can assign them to specific branches with granular roles:
   - **Branch Manager**: Full branch operations.
   - **Inventory Staff**: Stock counts and demo operations.
   - **Branch Viewer**: Branch-scoped read-only access.

---

## 3. Subscription & Billing Plans

You can view or upgrade your plan anytime under **Settings** > **Billing & Subscription**:

- **Free Trial**: 30 days full exploration, 1 branch.
- **Standard**: 3 branches, priority support.
- **Premium**: 10 branches, multi-location governance.
